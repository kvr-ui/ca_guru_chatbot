# CA Inter — Passing marks and exemption

Asked as: pass mark, passing marks ca inter, how much to pass, minimum marks, aggregate, exemption, 60 marks exemption, evlo mark vanganum pass aaga

To pass: about 40% in each paper and 50% aggregate across the group. A paper with 60 or more marks can be carried forward as an exemption. Always tell the student ICAI's current rules are final.
