# CA Inter — Eligibility / Who can join

Asked as: eligibility for ca inter, who can write inter, can graduate join ca inter, direct entry, foundation pass panna inter ezhudhalama, non commerce ca inter

Eligibility for CA Intermediate: pass CA Foundation (40% in each subject and 50% aggregate), or the direct-entry route for commerce or non-commerce graduates. For exact current rules, ICAI is the authority.
