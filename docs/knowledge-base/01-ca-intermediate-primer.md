# 1. CA Intermediate Program Primer

ICAI runs CA Intermediate under the **New Scheme**, with exams held **three times a year — January,
May, and September**. A student can register for and appear in **one group, or both groups
together, in a given attempt**.

| Group | Paper | Subject | Common nicknames |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group I | Paper 1 | Advanced Accounting | "Accounts" |
| Group I | Paper 2 | Corporate and Other Laws | "Law" |
| Group I | Paper 3 | Taxation (Direct Tax + Indirect Tax/GST) | "Tax", "DT", "IDT"/"GST" |
| Group II | Paper 4 | Cost and Management Accounting | "Costing", "CMA" |
| Group II | Paper 5 | Auditing and Ethics | "Audit" |
| Group II | Paper 6 | Financial Management & Strategic Management | "FM", "SM", "FM-SM" |

Eligibility is CA Foundation passed (40% per subject, 50% aggregate) or direct-entry
(commerce/non-commerce graduate route). Passing a paper requires roughly 40% in that paper with a
50% aggregate across the group — treat exact current passing/exemption rules as ICAI's authority,
not this document, if precision matters for a specific claim.

**A student may mention subjects instead of "Group 1/Group 2" outright** — use the table above to
map subject names to a group when they don't use the G1/G2 label directly (e.g., "I'm scared of
Costing and FM" → Group II).
