# 5. Practical Notes for the Chatbot

- **Audience is Tamil Nadu, Thanglish-heavy.** Expect Tamil-English code-switching, abbreviations
  (G1/G2, DT/IDT, "Inter", "attempt"), and casual/incomplete grammar. Don't require textbook
  English to extract a signal.
- **Don't guess on silence.** If a dimension truly isn't addressed anywhere in the conversation,
  mark it unclear/unassessed rather than defaulting to the most common value.
- **Ask one direct clarifying question at a time** when a dimension is ambiguous and matters for
  what happens next (e.g., routing to the right batch or content) — don't interrogate across all
  five dimensions in one go.
- **Keep tone warm and low-pressure.** These are exam-stressed students, often right after writing
  (or about to write) a hard exam — the classification should feel like a caring check-in, not a
  form to fill out.
- Repeaters frequently reference **one cleared group + one pending group** in the same breath —
  read the full message before assigning groups, since the "currently preparing for" group is
  usually what matters, not the historically cleared one.
