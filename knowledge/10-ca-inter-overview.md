# CA Intermediate — Overview (groups, papers, exam months)

Asked as: what is ca inter, ca inter details, how many groups, how many papers, ca inter structure, inter pathi sollunga, inter la evlo paper, new scheme

CA Intermediate is conducted by ICAI under the New Scheme. It has 6 papers in 2 groups (3 papers each). Exams are held three times a year: January, May and September. A student can write one group or both groups together in an attempt.
